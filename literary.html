Understanding Multimodal Literacy

A multimodal text combines two or more communication modes to convey a unified meaning. It merges written language, visual imagery, and structural design to make information more engaging and accessible. For our digital expo, we have crafted a multimodal presentation that bridges scientific facts about electromagnetic radiation with practical consumer budgeting to advocate for holistic community wellness.
