Demand and Supply Concepts

In a free-market economy, prices are determined by the interaction of buyers and sellers. Recognizing these forces helps you budget your household income effectively:

  • The Law of Demand: As the price of a healthy good decreases, consumer demand increases.
  • The Law of Supply: As the market price increases, producers supply more items to maximize profit.
  • Market Equilibrium: This occurs when quantity demanded matches the exact quantity supplied.
