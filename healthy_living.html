Lifestyle Guidelines for Wellness

Achieving a healthy life requires consistency across physical nutrition, daily exercise, and mental rejuvenation:

  • Nutrient-Dense Diet: Prioritize whole foods like leafy greens, lean proteins, and complex carbohydrates.
  • Physical Activity: Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily to support cardiovascular health.
  • Hydration and Rest: Drink a minimum of 8 glasses of water daily and secure 7 to 9 hours of sleep.
  • Digital Detoxing: Establish dedicated device-free hours before bedtime to limit non-ionizing blue light.
SYSTEM CHECK: Following these foundational pillars regularly fosters both physical and digital wellness in our hyper-connected local community.
