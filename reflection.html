Personal Reflection Portfolio

Working on this multi-disciplinary project has deeply broadened our understanding of how much choosing to do morally good and healthy actions can impact everyday life. We discovered that the choices we make—whether it is buying local produce, managing our screen time, or understanding invisible electromagnetic waves—have direct long-term consequences on our personal health and household finances. True wellness requires taking a conscious look answering how we behave both offline and online.

DIGITAL_CITIZENSHIP_FRAMEWORK.EXE 🗙

The Digital Citizenship Framework

"A responsible digital citizen does not just surf the web safely; they maintain a balanced lifestyle in a tech-driven world."
Pillar of Digital Citizenship Personal Daily Action Item Community Health Impact
Digital Health & Wellness Setting strict time limits on social media and taking regular screen breaks to protect eyesight. Reduces mental fatigue, prevents insomnia, and lowers direct non-ionizing blue light exposure.
Digital Responsibility Verifying scientific facts before sharing articles about data networks or health trends online. Prevents the spread of medical misinformation and builds an informed local neighborhood.
Digital Security Using strong, distinct passwords and keeping background device location trackers switched off. Protects personal identity and keeps student data secure from external online tracking.

Affirmation of Digital Citizenship

"As active participants of the modern digital landscape, we firmly pledge to use technology mindfully and ethically. We commit to managing our digital footprint, protecting our physical health from excessive device reliance, and respecting market dynamics as informed consumers. We will use our online voices to uplift others, spread validated information, and champion balanced, sustainable living within our school, home, and global communities."
